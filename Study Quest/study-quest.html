    HOW TO PLAY
    Pick a subject and write what this block is for, then set your study and break lengths and your daily goal in hours. Press A to start. When a block ends, rate your focus 1–3 (keys 1, 2, 3 work too). The log book below shows today's hours per subject and every block you finished. Every minute you study fills the Daily Quest bar and earns 1 XP. Breaks start on their own when a block ends. Hit 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of your goal to collect stars, and meet your goal on back-to-back days to build a streak. Keyboard: Space = A, R = B. Progress is saved in this browser.
